Search a huge selection!

Our application searches all of the movies in IMDB's database, allowing you to find your film in a matter of seconds! Search results are accompanied by release year and film posters where possible, helping you find your particular film in a sea of endless remakes and reboots.

We quickly provide you with all the information you need to decide with certainty if a movie is worth your time! You can even skip straight to the details if you know your movie's IMDB ID already.
